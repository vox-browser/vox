# Video track lists notify their media owner

`HTMLMediaElement.create()` now constructs its `VideoTrackList` with the creating media owner. The existing owner callback then recomputes natural dimensions after insertion and after clearing the list. Previously, those membership changes could leave poster dimensions cached after video became available, or leave retained video paint after the final track disappeared. The callback also republishes paint facts when dimensions are equal, allowing representation changes without rebuilding layout.

This follows upstream `963d46a829f3e1b79f44dba9d02afb70654300fa`, `HTML/HTMLMediaElement.cpp`'s `VideoTrackList::create(this)` and `HTML/VideoTrackList.cpp`'s owner notification after append/association and after deselection/detachment/clear. The V callback updates an already-materialized video model and otherwise remains a no-op, matching the base media-element behavior. Ownerless lists retain their previous behavior; this adds no track events or selection changes.
